Our commitment to you

We do not sell your data. We do not collect your brokerage passwords or account numbers. All financial information you enter is voluntary and used only to personalize your DividendIQ experience.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect: your name, email address, and the financial profile information you voluntarily enter (age, portfolio value, income goal, brokerage name, Social Security estimate). We do not collect your actual brokerage account credentials, account numbers, or Social Security number. We also collect basic usage data (pages visited, features used) to improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: (a) provide and personalize the Service — your financial profile is used to calculate your DividendIQ Retirement Score and AI recommendations; (b) process subscription payments via Stripe; (c) send you account-related emails and, if you opt in, dividend alerts and weekly summaries; (d) improve the platform based on aggregate usage patterns. We do not use your personal financial data to train AI models.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your account data is stored securely using Supabase, a SOC 2 certified database platform. Financial profile data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. Payment information is handled exclusively by Stripe and is never stored on our servers. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure.

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect: your brokerage login credentials, actual brokerage account numbers, Social Security numbers, tax identification numbers, government-issued ID, or any data from your actual investment accounts. Portfolio data you enter is self-reported and voluntary.

5. Third-Party Services

DividendIQ Pro uses the following third-party services: Finnhub (market data), Anthropic Claude (AI analysis), Stripe (payments), and Supabase (database). Each service has its own privacy policy. We share only the minimum necessary data with each provider to deliver the Service.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We do not share your financial profile data with advertisers. We may share information with service providers (listed above) solely to operate the Service. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

7. Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal cookies necessary to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate usage patterns (pages visited, features used). We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect your ability to use the Service.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to: (a) access the personal data we hold about you; (b) correct inaccurate data; (c) delete your account and all associated data — use the Delete Account option in Settings; (d) export your data — contact support@dividendiq.info; (e) opt out of marketing emails at any time via the unsubscribe link in any email we send.

9. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we permanently delete your profile, watchlist, and personal data within 30 days. Aggregate, anonymized usage statistics may be retained indefinitely.

10. Children's Privacy

DividendIQ Pro is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at support@dividendiq.info and we will delete it promptly.

11. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under the CCPA, including the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your CCPA rights, contact support@dividendiq.info.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated via email or in-app notification at least 14 days before taking effect. Your continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, data requests, or concerns, contact us at: support@dividendiq.info. We respond to all privacy inquiries within 5 business days.
